# My Favorite Anime: Re:Zero − Starting Life in Another World

## Why I Enjoy This Anime
**Re:Zero** is one of the most emotionally intense isekai anime I’ve ever watched.  
It beautifully explores *struggle*, *resilience*, and *character growth* through the journey of Natsuki Subaru.

## What Makes Re:Zero Special
1. Deep and complex characters  
2. Unique time-loop mechanic (Return by Death)  
3. Strong world-building and lore  
4. High emotional impact
